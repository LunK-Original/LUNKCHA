3 controllers, in one download

LUNKCHA.zip has everything: 2D, first person 3D, and third person 3D. Just extract, import into your project, and go.

● 2D · CharacterBody2D

2D Platformer Controller

A classic 2D controller that already feels "right" out of the box — run, jump, coyote time, all parameters exposed in the Inspector.

  • Coyote time & jump buffering
  • Variable jump height
  • Wall slide, wall jump & ledge grab

● 3D · CharacterBody3D

First Person 3D Controller

Walking, running, jumping, and look-around that already feel polished. Easy to extend for FPS or exploration games.

  • Mouse look + gamepad support
  • Sprint, crouch, jump smoothing
  • Automatic slope & stair handling

● 3D · CharacterBody3D

Third Person 3D Platformer Controller

A finely tuned spring-arm camera: no wall clipping, no jerky motion. Perfect for 3D platformers, adventure, or action games.

  • Anti-clipping spring-arm camera
  • Rotate-to-move & strafe mode
  • Jump, double jump, coyote time
